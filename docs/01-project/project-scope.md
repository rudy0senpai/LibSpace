# Project Scope
**Status:** Planned

## Must have (MVP)
Authentication · Role-based dashboard · Book catalogue · Search/filter/sort · Book availability · Physical book copies · Borrow/return · Suggestions · Voting · Librarian dashboard · Notifications (in-app)

## Strong enhancements
Reservations · Waiting list · QR/barcode · Fine calculation · Analytics · Audit logs

## Future scope
AI recommendations · Natural-language search · Email notifications · Library map · PWA/mobile · College SSO · Advanced analytics

## Explicitly not prioritized initially
Online payment gateway · facial recognition · blockchain · full AI chatbot · RFID hardware · native Android/iOS apps · complex microservices. Also no Redis or Elasticsearch unless real requirements justify them.

> A smaller polished system is better for the hackathon than a large incomplete one. Do not expand the MVP before the core works.
